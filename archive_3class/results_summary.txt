    CNN   100.00%

  Low-OSNR regime (5-10 dB), mean accuracy:
    CNN   89.04%
    SVM   72.15%
    kNN   68.15%

  (The CNN's advantage is concentrated in the low-OSNR regime: when the
   constellation is clean, 15 hand-picked statistics are enough, but
   when it is buried in noise the full 2-D density image carries
   information that those statistics throw away.)
